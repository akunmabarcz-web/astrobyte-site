Frequently asked questions

Is there a sign-up or a login?

No. There is no account system, no registration and no login page. The tool authorises through the channel owner's own Google account on the owner's own computer, and nobody else has access to it. Because there is no login, there are no test credentials to provide.

Can anyone else use it?

No. Astrobyte is built for one channel and one operator. It is not distributed, not offered as a service, and not licensed to third parties.

What data does it collect?

It does not collect data from anyone. Everything it reads belongs to the channel it operates: that channel's own videos, its own statistics, and comments left on its own videos. There is no third-party user data because there are no third-party users.

Does it post replies to viewers automatically?

No. Replies are written as drafts and the channel owner reads and approves each one before it is posted. The only automatic moderation action is hiding obvious spam.

Does it use my YouTube account if I watch the channel?

No. Nothing the tool does involves viewers or their accounts. It has no access to viewing data of individual people.

Is it affiliated with YouTube or Google?

No. Astrobyte is an independent tool built by the channel owner. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by YouTube or Google. It uses Google's public APIs under the owner's own authorisation.

Why does the site exist if the tool is private?

Because the tool connects to Google's APIs, it needs the standard public pages: what the tool does, how it works, a privacy policy and terms. These pages are that documentation. They are also the pages the app review process looks at, which is why the app icon, the icon in the header and the browser tab icon are all the same file.

Where can I read the detailed documents?

  • Privacy Policy
  • Terms of Service
