Astrobyte

A desktop automation tool for the Astro and Tech YouTube channel.

Astrobyte runs on the channel owner's own computer and automates the channel's own workflow: scheduling and uploading videos to the channel, reading the channel's own statistics (views, subscribers, comments, watch time), and hiding obvious spam comments.

What it is

  • A single-user tool, operated by the owner of @astroandtech.
  • It uses the owner's own Google authorisation (OAuth) to talk to the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API.
  • It has no public sign-up, no user accounts, and no third-party users.

What it is not

  • Not a public service or a website you log into.
  • Not a data collection product — it collects no data from anyone.
  • Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by YouTube or Google.
